About

Alignment before execution

Working Method Flexible

Regional Expertise DACH

Hebel is a strategic advisory firm supporting companies in building the right partnerships, securing suitable funding, and structuring high-value collaborations.

We work with technology-driven businesses, particularly in electronics, data, and innovation-heavy sectors, helping them translate internal capabilities into concrete commercial and strategic opportunities.

Our work is centered around the DACH region, while supporting cross-border collaborations where strategic value is created.

Engagements are structured based on project complexity and objectives, ranging from focused strategic phases to multi-month execution support.

Market Research

Before engaging in funding or collaboration, structural clarity is essential.

  • Market environment and industry dynamics
  • Competitive landscape overview
  • Positioning and differentiation assessment
  • Opportunity mapping
  • Regulatory and policy conditions

We analyze markets, value chains, regulatory environments, and competitive positioning to determine where initiatives are realistically viable — and where they are not.

Partner Sourcing

The strength of a project often depends on the strength of its partners.

We identify and qualify partners aligned with clearly defined objectives and execution capacity, including:

  • Research institutions and universities
  • Industrial and technology partners
  • Commercial and market-facing entities
  • Capability and credibility assessment
  • Strategic and operational alignment

This is a central component of programmes such as Horizon Europe and Eurostars, where consortium credibility directly influences positioning and evaluation.

At the same time, partner selection is not limited to funding contexts. Strategic collaboration — whether within public programmes or independent industrial initiatives — often determines long-term viability.

For this reason, Partner Sourcing can be delivered as a standalone service or integrated into a broader funding engagement.

Collaboration Design

When participating in multi-party projects, clarity of position is essential.

We support companies in defining their role within the broader project framework, ensuring alignment between:

  • Technical contribution
  • Overall project objectives
  • Work package responsibilities
  • Expected outcomes and impact

We help articulate where your expertise fits within the consortium logic — and how it should be presented.

Execution Support

Once structure and positioning are clear, formal documentation must reflect that clarity.

We prepare structured proposals aligned with selected funding instruments, ensuring coherence between strategic intent and formal requirements.

Support includes:

  • Technical narrative drafting
  • Impact and implementation sections
  • Budget preparation and cost structuring
  • Submission coordination

The objective is consistency — between what the project intends to achieve and how it is formally presented.

Project Coordination

Approval is not the end of the process.

Structured implementation is essential to preserve credibility and ensure long-term benefit.

We support companies in maintaining clarity throughout execution, including:

  • Timeline and milestone alignment
  • Reporting structure and documentation flows
  • Responsibility mapping
  • Communication and coordination discipline
  • Compliance oversight

The objective is controlled execution — transparent, aligned, and consistent with the original project logic.

Use Cases

You’re a diversified industrial company ready to launch something new

You build complex products, have strong production capabilities, and even some free capacity — but now you want to introduce a new, innovative product into the market through the right partners and channels.

We help you identify high-fit partners, design collaboration models, and make targeted introductions that turn capacity and innovation into real commercial traction.

You’ve launched a new product — now it needs real-world adoption (and funding)

You’re an early-stage company with a fresh, innovative solution, but getting it into real customer environments often requires pilots, integration partners, and funded projects that reduce risk for early adopters.

We help you structure pilot use cases, connect you with MSPs and strategic partners, and secure suitable funding to accelerate adoption and market validation.

You have a proven product — now you want to win more end customers

Your technology is established and reliable, but complex customer projects rarely close without integrators, MSPs, and solution partners delivering the full stack around it.

We help you build the right partner ecosystem, align roles and incentives, structure joint offerings, and orchestrate collaborations that turn opportunities into closed customer deals.

You know what your company can deliver, but transforming expertise into concrete market projects often depends on clear structure and the right financing.

We help you shape investment-ready initiatives, connect capabilities to real customer needs, and secure public or private funding to turn potential into execution.
