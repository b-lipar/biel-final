Biel supports companies that are entering new phases of growth — whether through market expansion, structured partnerships, investment initiatives, or participation in larger project environments.

We work at the intersection of strategy and execution, helping translate internal capabilities into structured, realistic initiatives. This often involves navigating complex environments: cross-border collaboration, multi-party projects, or international funding frameworks.

Our experience spans national and European project landscapes, as well as cooperation with partners across the DACH region and wider European markets. We understand both the formal requirements and the practical realities of operating in international settings.

The focus is not on isolated applications or short-term activity, but on building initiatives that are commercially meaningful and operationally sound.

Partner Sourcing

The strength of a project often depends on the strength of its partners.

We identify and qualify partners aligned with clearly defined objectives and execution capacity, including:

  • Research institutions and universities
  • Industrial and technology partners
  • Commercial and market-facing entities
  • Capability and credibility assessment
  • Strategic and operational alignment

This is a central component of programmes such as Horizon Europe and Eurostars, where consortium credibility directly influences positioning and evaluation.

At the same time, partner selection is not limited to funding contexts. Strategic collaboration, whether within public programmes or independent industrial initiatives, often determines long-term viability.

For this reason, Partner Sourcing can be delivered as a standalone service or integrated into a broader funding engagement.

How We Work

You are evaluating a new market or product direction

Before discussing funding, we clarify whether the initiative is commercially and structurally viable.

This may include market and competitive landscape analysis, value chain positioning, regulatory assessment, and early-stage funding relevance screening. The objective is clarity on whether the opportunity justifies structured investment — with or without public funding.

You have internal R&D or investment plans — and want to explore funding

We assess how national and European instruments can support your development roadmap.

This includes mapping relevant SPS and SPIRIT calls, evaluating Horizon Europe and Eurostars fit, assessing whether EIC participation is realistic, and aligning timing and eligibility constraints. The outcome is a coherent funding pathway aligned with internal priorities — not isolated applications.

You need the right partners — with or without a funding call

We identify and qualify research, industrial, and commercial partners based on strategic and operational fit.

This may support Horizon Europe participation, Eurostars project formation, or independent industrial cooperation. Partner Sourcing can also be delivered as a standalone engagement. The focus is structured selection — capability, compatibility, and alignment before formal commitment.

You are preparing a formal application — or moving into execution

We translate strategic intent into structured documentation aligned with funding requirements.

This includes Horizon Europe proposal sections, Eurostars applications, SPS R&D documentation, and budget structuring. For approved projects, we support implementation discipline through timeline alignment, reporting structure, and governance coordination to ensure controlled and credible execution.
